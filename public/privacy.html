Privacy

last updated: june 2026

Designed around not knowing you

Ecosphere is anonymous by design. We don't ask for your name, photo, birthdate, phone number, or location, and the app has no way to search for or contact a specific person.

What lives on your device

Most of your data never leaves your browser: your signal identity, preferences, listening history, recordings (IndexedDB), saved signals, and customizations are stored locally. "Clear Local Data" in Settings erases all of it.

What the backend stores (when connected)

When a backend is configured, the service stores: an anonymous account id, your chosen signal name, recordings you make (private by default), your saves/replays/reactions, notifications, and your profile customizations (bio tape, colors). Audio files live in private storage accessed via short-lived signed links. Row-level security restricts every record to its owner or to explicitly public content.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no sale of data, no third-party trackers.
  • No contact lists, no social graph imports.
  • No reading of private content by humans — moderation is automated.

Deletion

Settings → Erase Cloud Data deletes your recordings, signals, reactions, notifications, subscriptions, and profile from the backend, then signs you out. Settings → Clear Local Data wipes this device. Together they remove everything you've made.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you press record (or explicitly start calibration), and the permission is requested only at that moment. Nothing is captured in the background.

Questions

This service stores the minimum it can. If a future feature changes that, this page changes first.

Privacy

last updated: october 2026

Plain version: Ecosphere is anonymous, stores as little as it can, never sells anything, and lets you delete it all. The rest of this page is the detail.

Designed around not knowing you

Ecosphere is anonymous by design. We don't ask for your name, photo, birthdate, phone number, or location, and the app has no way to search for or contact a specific person. If you sign in with Google, your email is used only to keep your account yours — it is never shown to other people or tied to your public signal.

What lives on your device

Most of your data never leaves your browser: your signal identity, preferences, listening history, recordings (IndexedDB), saved signals, and customizations are stored locally. "Clear Local Data" in Settings erases all of it.

What the backend stores (when connected)

When a backend is configured, the service stores: an anonymous account id, your chosen signal name, recordings you make (private by default), your saves/replays/reactions, notifications, and your profile customizations (intro tape, colors, atmosphere). Audio files live in private storage reached through short-lived signed links. Row-level security restricts every record to its owner or to content you've deliberately made public.

Your voice & recordings

Voice recordings are content — the thing you chose to make — not a biometric identifier. We do not create voiceprints, we do not use your voice to recognize or identify you or anyone else, and we do not run voice biometrics. Recordings are private until you choose to share them. Voice clips shared into a group are transcribed only to screen them for safety before others can hear them; the transcript is used for moderation, not profiling.

We don't profile you

We don't analyze, score, or draw conclusions from what you say or record. We don't build profiles about you — not from your voice, not from what you talk about, not from how you use the app. There's no advertising or targeting, and nothing here is sold or shared. Whatever you make, you can delete (see Deletion).

Site analytics & performance

To see how many people visit and whether pages load quickly, the app uses Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights, provided by our hosting company. They use no cookies and collect no names, account ids, recordings, or anything you write. Each data point records things like the page address, referrer, time, browser, operating system, device type, and an approximate region (country / state / city) derived from the connection; Speed Insights adds page-load timing. Visitors are counted using a short-lived hash of the request that is discarded after 24 hours, and we only see aggregated totals. This data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to profile you.

Fonts

The app's typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's font servers, which receive your IP address and browser details as part of that request.

Reports

When you report a signal, the backend stores the reason you picked, your anonymous account id, and a copy of the reported signal's text along with the anonymous id of the account that posted it, so the report can still be reviewed and repeat abuse acted on even if the signal or either account is later deleted. Reports are not visible to other users, including the person you reported, and are kept only as long as needed for safety review.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no sale or "sharing" of data, no advertising or cross-site tracking, and no analytics beyond the aggregated, cookieless site statistics described above.
  • No contact lists, no social-graph imports, no followers, no likes.
  • No human reading of your private content. Public signals that someone reports may be reviewed by a person.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (including California and other U.S. states with privacy laws, and "consumer health data" laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. Because we don't sell or share data or run targeted ads, there's nothing to opt out of — but the access and deletion tools are built in (Settings), and you can also email us. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Deletion

Settings → Erase Cloud Data deletes your recordings, signals, reactions, notifications, subscriptions, and profile from the backend, then signs you out. Settings → Clear Local Data wipes this device. Together they remove everything you've made, except copies of content that someone else reported, which are kept for safety review as described under Reports. You can also email us to request deletion.

Live voice channels

Live voice channels are not recorded or stored. Audio travels directly between the devices in a channel (peer-to-peer). Because of that, the other people in a channel can technically see your device's network (IP) address, which can reveal your approximate area. You see this notice before your first live channel, and you can stay out of live channels entirely. Live audio is not screened in advance; you can mute or report any voice, and reports are stored as described under Reports, without any audio.

Wavelength matching

While you're on the grid, the app shares an anonymous "wavelength" with other people who are online at the same time, so you can find people who feel like you tonight: the signal mood and energy you picked, your frequency (Hz), the hour it is where you are, the kinds of signals you replay most, and your sigil and color. It goes out under a random key that changes every session. It never includes your account id, your call sign, your email, or anything you've recorded, and it isn't stored: it disappears when you close the app. Waves and tune-ins are sent to that random key only. You can turn matching off any time (inbox → "go unmatched"), and then you're neither matched nor shown matches.

AI vibe read

If you use "read my vibe" while setting up your node, the three answers you tap and the optional line you type are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to suggest your colors, sigil and backdrop. They aren't stored by us or used for anything else, and nothing about you is attached. If the AI is unavailable, the suggestion is made on your device instead.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you press record, start calibration, or turn on "let it hear you" for your 3D sigil (which reacts to your voice's loudness on your device and never records or sends audio), and the permission is requested only at that moment. Nothing is captured in the background.

Children

Ecosphere is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect anything from anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to a minor, we remove it. If you believe a minor is using the service, please report it in-app or email us.

Where this applies

Ecosphere is operated from the United States and intended for users there. If you use it from elsewhere, you're responsible for local law, and you consent to your data being handled in the U.S.

Changes & contact

This service stores the minimum it can. If a future feature changes that, this page changes first, and material changes are shown in the app. Privacy questions or requests: privacy@ecosphere.app.
